FAQ

Good questions, honest answers

What if I need to cancel a date?

Life happens. Cancel up to 24 hours before and your commitment fee rolls over to your next date. Late cancellations and no-shows forfeit the fee.

Is it safe to meet someone without chatting first?

Safer than most apps. Everyone is ID-verified, dates are in public partner venues, we confirm check-ins, and our support team is available throughout your date.

How much does FLIK2 cost?

Downloading and matching is free. You only pay the small per-date commitment fee, which covers your reservation and first round.
