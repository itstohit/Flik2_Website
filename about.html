About us

Technology should bring people together, not keep them apart

FLIK2 was born from a simple frustration: dating apps had turned love into a numbers game of swipes and dead-end chats. We decided to build the opposite.

Our mission

Humanise online dating

We believe real connection happens in person: across a table, over a coffee, in the middle of a laugh you didn't see coming. So we removed everything that keeps people stuck behind a screen and kept only what gets them face to face.

No endless chats. No ghosting. No games. Just one honest question: are you willing to take the leap?

Every date booked on FLIK2 supports local cafes, bars, and restaurants in your city, so each leap of faith also gives something back to your neighbourhood.

What we stand for

Three promises we don't break

🤝

Real people, always

We read every message and handle every support request ourselves. When you contact FLIK2, you're always talking to a human, never a bot.

🛡

Safety before growth

ID verification, public venues, and live check-ins are non-negotiable. We'd rather grow slower than compromise on how safe a date feels.

🌎

Offline beats online

We measure success in dates that happen, not minutes spent in the app. The best session on FLIK2 is a short one that ends at a cafe table.
